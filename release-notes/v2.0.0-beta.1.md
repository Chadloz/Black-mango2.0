# Black Mango 2.0, test build 1

The first test build of the new Black Mango, rebuilt from the ground up on a faster video engine.

## What's in it

- Live TV, Movies and Shows from Xtream, M3U, and Stalker (MAG) portal accounts
- Smart paste: paste your provider's message and the account fills itself in
- Failover: if a channel, movie or episode won't play, it switches to the same one on another of your accounts
- Smoother live TV: seamless reconnects, extra buffer for channels that stutter, and quick switching away from dead channels
- A mini player that floats over other apps, with a slim minimized bar
- Clean titles and channel names, with small quality and language tags
- Remembers your audio and subtitle choices for each show
- Backup and restore for accounts, favorites, history and settings
- Automatic updates

## Good to know

- Mac only for now. Windows is coming.
- This is a test build. If something misbehaves, send the log from **Settings > Debug Log > Copy recent**.

# Black Mango 2.0, test build 8

A stability build: fixes from the bug log, before 2.0.

## Steadier live TV

- **Reconnects hold together better.** When a provider went quiet, the player sometimes gave up while Black Mango was still reconnecting, and the channel restarted or was marked down. The player now waits for the reconnect to finish.
- **Backups stay close to what you picked:** the same country first, then channels with no country label, and another country only as the last resort. The message tells you when that happens.
- **Error 458 is handled right away.** When a provider refuses a channel with error 458, Black Mango goes straight to a backup. If there isn't one, it tells you what 458 can mean: the account is in use somewhere else, or the provider is blocking that channel for now.
- A channel whose server no longer exists fails right away, without 14 seconds of retries.
- **Starved channels are dropped quickly.** When a provider sends almost no video and keeps closing the connection, Black Mango used to reconnect for about a minute. Now it goes to a backup after two rounds, or tells you the provider is sending almost nothing.
- One kind of portal was asked for a check-in it doesn't have every two minutes. It's asked once now.
- Messages on the player wrap onto more lines. Long ones used to run off the edge.

## Live TV list

- The three highlights look different from each other: the **playing** channel keeps its tint and sound bars, the **keyboard's** row is an outline, and the **mouse** keeps its soft bar. Only one of those shows at a time: while you use the arrow keys the mouse bar hides, and moving the mouse clears the outline.
- When a backup is playing, the channel you picked shows a small **PICKED** tag. It no longer looks like a second playing channel.

## New

- **Favorite star on the player.** Click the star on the control bar, or press **S**, to add what's playing to your favorites or take it back out. The shortcut can be changed in Settings.

## Accounts

- **Reload all:** one button reloads every account, one after another. **Stop all** ends it.
- **Stop:** an account that's loading has a Stop button. Its saved channels stay as they were.
- **Slow portals don't trap you.** When a portal is answering very slowly, Black Mango tries its full channel list once more, and if it's still crawling, keeps the channels you already have and says so, instead of loading for 45 minutes.

## Free channels

- **Packs refresh themselves** once a day in the background, so their links stay current.
- **New packs:** Pluto TV, TCL Plus, TCL TV, Vidaa, Local Now, Tablo, and LG Channels US.
- **Fixed packs:** Samsung TV Plus and Roku Channel had stopped working; both load again. If you already added them, they fix themselves at the next refresh.
- **Removed:** DistroTV, whose channels no longer answer. If you added it, remove it in Accounts.

## For testers

- **Windows:** if Black Mango freezes for a while when it opens, the log now shows where the time went (lines starting with [startup] and [freeze]). Please copy the log from Settings after a slow start and send it. There's also a new switch to try: **Settings > Windows > Use the older drawing engine**.
- If the app stops responding for a moment, the [freeze] line in the log now says what it was waiting on. Account loads are in the log too ([load] lines).
- The log now notes when a channel's sound and picture are stamped far apart by the provider (the channel that plays silent, then freezes).

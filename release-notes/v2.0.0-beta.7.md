# Black Mango 2.0, test build 7

## New: make your own skins

- Skins now has its own page: **Settings > Skins**.
- **Make your own:** start from the skin you're using or from Black Mango, then pick colors. The whole app changes as you go, and nothing is kept until you save.
- **Simple** asks for three colors (background, panels and accent) and works out the rest. **Advanced** lets you set all of them, including the player buttons.
- Pick colors with a **color wheel**, by typing a **hex code**, or from the **built-in skins' colors**.
- **Pick from screen:** an eyedropper that takes a color from anything on your screen, even outside Black Mango.
- **Skin from a picture:** choose a picture and Black Mango suggests a skin in its colors. Then click the picture to take any color from it.
- **Textures:** grain, carbon fiber, brushed metal or paper, or your own picture. Give each area its own: the background, the top bar and sidebar, cards, search boxes, and highlights. Set each one's strength, size and tint. Your own picture can fill an area like a wallpaper or repeat as a pattern.
- If some text would be hard to read, Black Mango tells you when you save. You can go back, keep your colors, or let it fix just those colors.
- Add the **Halloween extras** to any skin: decorations, the dripping title font and the jack-o'-lantern logo.
- **My skins** keeps as many as you like. Share one as a **skin code** to paste anywhere, or as a **skin file** (the file includes your own picture; a code can't fit one). Paste a code or import a file to add someone else's. Pasting a skin code into Add Account works too.

## New: holiday skin drops

- Open Black Mango during a holiday and you collect that holiday's skin pack, yours for good. A gift box opens to show it off.
- **Thanksgiving** (November 1 through Thanksgiving Day): Harvest, Autumn Leaves, and Cranberry & Gold, with a mango turkey, leaves drifting down, a harvest of pumpkins and pie in the top bar, and a turkey who says Gobble! when you add an account.
- **Christmas** (the day after Thanksgiving through December 25): Classic Christmas, Snowy Night, and Candy Cane, with the mango in a Santa hat holding a candy cane, snow falling, twinkling lights along the top, a snowman, an elf and a reindeer, frosty corners, and a present that pops open when you add an account.
- Collected skins live in **My skins** with a star. They can't be shared as a code or file, but you can make a copy in their colors to edit.
- Watch for surprise drops, and giveaway codes: type one in **Settings > Skins > Redeem a code**.

## Safer

- The video player never hands links to other programs like yt-dlp.
- The behind-the-scenes relay that plays live channels now only answers the player on your own computer.
- Open in VLC only passes real stream links.
- **Settings > App updates** has a switch for automatic update checks. Updates are always signed, and you always choose whether to install.
- Each release now lists every file's checksum, and GitHub can confirm the files were built from the public code. See SECURITY.md in the repository.

## Fixed

- **Mouse users:** category strips in Live TV, Movies and Shows have a scrollbar, and the mouse wheel slides them. Rows on Home have a scrollbar too, and the wheel slides a row while you're over its scrollbar.
- The floating player no longer squeezes Live TV's guide into a tiny window behind it.

# Black Mango 2.0, test build 6

## New: skins

- **Settings > Appearance** has a new **Skin** picker. The whole app changes colors right away.
- **The Halloween pack:** Pumpkin Patch, Witching Hour, Blood Moon and Graveyard Fog. Each comes with a jack-o'-lantern mango, two flickering pumpkins and some bats in the top bar, cobwebs in the corners, a little fog, dripping titles, and a ghost who says Boo! when you add an account.
- Black Mango's own look is still the default skin, and you can switch back any time.

## Fixed

- Live TV, Movies and Shows keep your place in the list when the player floats or the window changes size. Only your own scrolling moves it.
- New **Top** and **Bottom** buttons on those lists, plus shortcuts: **Home** (top), **End** (bottom), and **B** to jump back to where you were.
